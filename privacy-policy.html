Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

Overview

Typing Pro ("we", "our", "the website") is a free online typing test and typing practice tool. This page explains what information is collected when you use the website and how it is used.

Information stored on your device

By default, Typing Pro stores your settings (theme, language, font size, WPM goal, etc.) and your typing test history directly in your browser's local storage. This information stays on your own device and is not sent anywhere unless you choose to create an account.

Account information (optional)

If you choose to create an account (via email/password or Google Sign-In), we use Firebase Authentication, a service provided by Google, to manage your login securely. If you sign in, your typing test history and settings may also be saved to a private cloud database (Firebase Firestore) so they can sync across your devices. Only you can access your own data — it is never shared with other users.

We collect only the information necessary to operate your account: your email address, and (for Google Sign-In) your name and profile photo if provided by Google. We do not sell or share this information with third parties.

Cookies and advertising

This website may display advertisements served by Google AdSense. Google and its partners may use cookies and similar technologies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this or other websites. You can learn more about how Google uses data and manage your ad preferences at Google's Advertising Policy page.

You can opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings.

Analytics

We may use standard web analytics tools to understand overall visitor traffic (such as number of visits and pages viewed). This data is aggregated and does not personally identify you.

Children's privacy

Typing Pro does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. The typing test itself can be used as a guest without any account or personal information at all.

Your choices

  • You can use all typing test and practice features as a guest without creating an account.
  • You can clear your locally stored data at any time using the "Reset all data" option in Settings.
  • You can delete your account data by contacting us at the email below.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.
